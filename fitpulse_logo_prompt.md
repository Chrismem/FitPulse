# Fitpulse Logo — AI Prompt & Test Result

## Product
**Fitpulse** — a fitness and health-tracking app for anyone working on their physical
health, whether that's weight loss, general fitness, or maintaining an active
lifestyle. The brand tone should be encouraging and inclusive, not focused on
body type or weight.

## Asset Type
Logo (primary product mark)

## Original Prompt (written by the team)

> Design a modern, minimalist logo for a fitness app called "Fitpulse."
> Combine a heartbeat/EKG pulse line with a subtle heart or upward-motion
> shape, so the mark reads as both "health monitoring" and "progress." Use a
> clean sans-serif wordmark next to the icon. Color palette: energetic teal
> (#0FB5AE) and warm coral-orange (#FF6B4A) on a white or transparent
> background. Style should feel encouraging, active, and inclusive — avoid
> any imagery referencing body size, scales, or weight loss specifically, so
> the brand welcomes people at any fitness level or starting point. Flat
> vector style, suitable for an app icon and website header, no gradients,
> no photorealism.

## Test / Result

The prompt was tested by generating a vector mark from the description above.
Result: `fitpulse_logo.svg` (included in this repo folder).

- Icon: a pulse/EKG line that peaks into a heart shape, rendered in teal and coral.
- Wordmark: "Fitpulse" in a clean, rounded sans-serif, teal with a coral accent dot.
- Format: SVG, scalable, transparent background — usable as app icon or header logo.
